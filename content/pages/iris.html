IRIS, the co-operating system for the internet.

Every app and website you use runs on rules someone else wrote. A co-operating system works on your side of those rules. It sits between you and everything you use, reads what each one says and watches what it does.

Get IRIS

IRIS reads the fine print for you.

Privacy policies and terms become seven plain answers about your rights, each with the sentence it came from: selling your data, deleting it, training AI on it, going to court, how long it's kept, where it goes, and children's data.

IRIS shows what's behind the page.

The companies a site talks to, who is identifying your browser, who reads what you type, and what the site does that its policy never mentions.

IRIS knows the company's record.

Hindsight shows what governments and courts have decided about the company behind a site or app, with every source named.

IRIS reads the code.

On a public code repository, a plain reading of what the software does with people's data, scored on the HAI Standard's nine pillars.

Everything is read on your device. IRIS sends nothing about you.

The one thing that can leave your device is an address you ask about: a code repository's or a website's, when you ask IRIS to read its code or look up a company. How IRIS reads

Get IRIS

Three places to run it.

IRIS Lite runs inside Firefox. IRIS Co-OS joins the operating system, on Linux now and GrapheneOS next. Both give the full readings. This section updates itself from IRIS's releases.

Firefox

IRIS Lite

In testing.

Works only inside Firefox: it reads the websites you visit there and sees nothing else on your device.

  1. Open IRIS on Firefox Add-ons.
  2. Press Add to Firefox, then Add.
  3. Pin the <•> to your toolbar and press it on any site.

Linux

IRIS Co-OS

In testing.

For Ubuntu and Debian on 64-bit Intel and AMD machines (amd64). Download the .deb, then in Terminal:

# install, then open IRIS sudo apt install ./omaro-iris_0.1.52_amd64.deb omaro-iris &

Check the file first: sha256sum omaro-iris_*_amd64.deb should print the number shown under the button.

GrapheneOS · recommended

IRIS Co-OS for your phone

Comes after Linux.

GrapheneOS is the most protected phone system IRIS runs on. IRIS will come through its own F-Droid repository and as a direct APK, never through Apple's or Google's stores.

  1. Add IRIS's repository in F-Droid or Obtainium, or download the APK.
  2. Check the signing fingerprint with AppVerifier against the one on this page.

From a computer, with the phone connected:

adb install iris-grapheneos.apk
