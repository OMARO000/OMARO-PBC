What OMARO is

A company bound to the people it serves.

OMARO is a public benefit corporation. Its charter names a public purpose beside profit: returning to people the authority that technology has taken over their attention, their data and their choices.

The dot in our mark is the person. The brackets are the frame technology builds around them, left open so there is room to act. Keeping that frame open is the work.
